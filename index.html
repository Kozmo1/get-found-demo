Our story

A neighbourhood hearth on Peel Street

Hearth & Crumb started as a tiny morning bake for New Hamburg neighbours — wild yeast, local flour, and coffee that doesn’t rush you. We’re still small on purpose: one oven, one street, and bread that tastes like someone cared.
